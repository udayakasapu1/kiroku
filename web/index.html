Web inbox: links you save here appear on your phone

Connect this browser

This page needs a one-time setup link from your phone.

  1. In Kiroku on your phone, open ⋮ → Web inbox.
  2. Tap Connect a browser and send the link to this computer (email, chat, notes…).
  3. Open that link here. That's it.

Save a link

Waiting for your phone

Nothing waiting. Saved links move to your phone the next time Kiroku checks (every ~15 minutes, or when you open it).
